0 FOMO Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Publisher: A.R.C Technology, Nassau, The Bahamas

0 FOMO is a local events app. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. The short version: the app does not require an account, only ever asks for approximate location (and works fine without it), and does not send us any personal information.

What the app collects

DataCollected?Notes
Name, email, phone, accountNoThere is no sign-in.
Precise locationNoThe app never requests precise (GPS) location.
Approximate locationOptional, on your device onlyIf you tap "Use my location", the app asks Android for a coarse (city-level) position, matches it against a list of cities built into the app, and keeps only the city name. The coordinates are not stored and nothing is sent to us. You can type a city instead and never grant the permission.
Saved events and remindersStored on your device onlyNever uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.
Device identifiers, advertising IDNoThe app shows no ads and contains no ad SDKs.
Analytics or crash reportsNot at this timeIf we add crash reporting, this policy will be updated first and the data will be anonymous.

Network activity

The app downloads a public events feed from our servers over HTTPS. That request carries the standard information any web request carries (your IP address and app version) and is used only to deliver the feed. We do not log or profile it.

When you open a ticket link, a venue website or a map, you leave the app and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders for events you saved. Reminders are generated on your device; no notification service receives your saved events.

Calendar export

"Add to calendar" hands the event to the calendar app you choose. 0 FOMO does not read your calendar.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). Listings may include nightlife and licensed venues.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in Android settings at any time.
  • Revoke the location permission in Android settings at any time; the app keeps working with the city you choose.
  • Clear saved events by removing them in the app or uninstalling it.

Changes

We will post any change to this page and update the effective date above.
