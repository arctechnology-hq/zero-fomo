0 FOMO is the events feed for people who hate finding out afterwards.

Concerts, parties, festivals, fairs, conferences, pop-ups and community events across New Providence, pulled together from ticket sites, venues and promoters into one clean list, refreshed every few hours. More islands and cities are coming.

WHAT YOU GET
• One feed. Ticket Flare, ETickets Live, Eventbrite, AllEvents, Bandsintown, Songkick, Reggaeville and more, de-duplicated so the same show is not listed four times.
• Filters that matter. Tonight, this weekend, next 30 days. By island. By category. By keyword.
• Works offline. The feed is cached on your phone, so the list still opens on the road or in a dead zone.
• Save it. Keep the events you care about and get a reminder before they start.
• Add to calendar. Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app that reads .ics files.
• Get tickets. One tap to the official ticket page, never a resale middleman.
• Share it. Send an event to a friend with a link that opens straight in the app.
• Send it in. See a flyer or a post about an event we missed? Share it to 0 FOMO from any app and it goes to our review queue.

WHAT YOU DO NOT GIVE UP
• No account. No sign-in.
• Location is optional and approximate. Pick your city yourself, or let the app use a coarse city-level position that never leaves your phone.
• No ads, no tracking, no data sold. Read the privacy policy: it fits on one screen.
