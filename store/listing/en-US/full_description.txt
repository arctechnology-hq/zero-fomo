0 FOMO is the events feed for people who hate finding out afterwards.

Concerts, parties, festivals, fairs, regattas, conferences, pop-ups and community events, pulled together from ticket sites, venues, tourism calendars and promoters into one clean list, refreshed every few hours.

WHERE IT WORKS
• The Bahamas: Nassau and Paradise Island, Grand Bahama, Abaco, Eleuthera and Harbour Island, Exuma, Bimini.
• The Caribbean: Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Puerto Rico, Cayman, Bermuda, Turks and Caicos, Anguilla, the Virgin Islands, Antigua, St. Kitts, Dominica, Grenada, Martinique, Montserrat, Haiti, the Dominican Republic, Bonaire, Curaçao, Sint Maarten, Guyana and more.
• Florida and the Southeast: Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Orlando, Atlanta.
• Mexico and Central America: Mexico City, San José, Panama City, Managua, San Salvador, Tegucigalpa.
New markets open as sources are found. If your island is thin today, the app widens to the nearest city or region on its own.

WHAT YOU GET
• One feed. Eventbrite, Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, AllEvents, Tikkets, Caribtix, Ticket Flare, ETickets Live, TriniJungleJuice, Reggaeville, bahamas.com and dozens of venue and community calendars, de-duplicated so the same show is not listed four times.
• Filters that matter. Tonight, this weekend, next 30 days. Near you, by island or city, by region, by category, by keyword.
• Works offline. The feed is cached on your phone, so the list still opens on the road or in a dead zone.
• Save it. Keep the events you care about and get a reminder before they start.
• Add to calendar. Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app that reads .ics files.
• Get tickets. One tap to the official ticket page, never a resale middleman.
• Share it. Send an event to a friend with a link that opens straight in the app.
• Send it in. See a flyer or a post about an event we missed? Share it to 0 FOMO from any app and it goes to our review queue.
• Your language. English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Haitian Creole and Dutch.

WHAT YOU DO NOT GIVE UP
• No account. No sign-in.
• Location is optional and approximate. Pick your city yourself, or let the app use a coarse city-level position that never leaves your phone.
• No ads, no tracking, no data sold. Read the privacy policy: it fits on one screen.
